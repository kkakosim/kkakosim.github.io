## Mass Transfer in Environmental Processes (in Greek)
Assael M.J., Kakosimos K.E., Antoniadis K.D., Panagiotopoulos K.P, 2012

[Publisher](https://www.tziola.gr/book/metamz/)

The book deals with mass transfer operations employed in the calculation of poluttants concentration in the atmosphere, in rivers, in lakes and in ground water. This is a limited edition written in Greek specifically for the students of the 4th year Environmental Mass Transfer course of the Chemical Engineering curriculum in Aristotle University.
