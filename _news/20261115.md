Our research team (Alexandros Dimakas, Louiza Christidou, Maria-Stefania Charitonidou) was selected and participated at the finals of the 1st FoodTech Hackaton organized by the Perfecture of Central Macedonia, Business Seeds by the National Bank of Greece, Alexadrian Innovation Zone SA, Bayer Healls, Microsoft, Walk by AUTh, and the Helexpo. We presented our idea for an adaptable disinfection system and won one of the honorary awards, although not the competition.
